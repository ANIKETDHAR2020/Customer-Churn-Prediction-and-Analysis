# Customer Churn Analysis 📊

An end-to-end Customer Churn Analysis project developed using **Python, SQL, SQLite, and Pandas** to analyze customer behavior, identify churn patterns, calculate business KPIs, and generate actionable business insights.

> **Note:** This project was completed as a guided learning project by following the YouTube tutorial *"Complete Data Analytics Portfolio Project | Learn How Data Analyst Work (Python + SQL)"*. The implementation, analysis, and documentation were completed as part of my learning journey.

---

## 📌 Project Objectives

- Analyze customer churn trends
- Build a relational SQLite database
- Perform data cleaning and transformation
- Calculate key business KPIs
- Generate business insights
- Recommend customer retention strategies

## 📂 Database Schema

The project uses three relational tables connected through **customerid**.

![Database Schema](Documentation/database_tables.png)

| Table | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **db_customer** | Customer demographic information |
| **db_subscription** | Subscription details and churn information |
| **db_support** | Customer support interactions |

## 📈 Key Metrics

- Churn Rate
- Retention Rate
- ARPU (Average Revenue Per User)
- Average Customer Tenure
- Revenue at Risk
- Escalation Rate
- Average Complaints per Customer

![Calculated Metrics](Documentation/calculated_metrics.png)

---

## 💡 Key Insights

- Overall churn rate: **28.6%**
- Retention rate: **71.4%**
- Highest churn observed in the **Basic** subscription plan.
- Karnataka recorded the highest customer churn.
- Monthly subscriptions showed significantly higher churn than annual subscriptions.
- Approximately **18%** of total revenue is at risk due to customer churn.
